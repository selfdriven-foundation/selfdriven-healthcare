Who it's for

Three disciplines. One ecosystem.

Most people's care crosses more than one discipline. The tools should too.

What we build

Protocols, services and tools.

Three layers that work on their own and work better together.

Protocols

Shared rules for trust.

How practitioners, patients and systems prove who they are and what was agreed.

  • Verifiable practitioner credentials

    Registration and scope of practice as credentials anyone can check, anchored with KERI and ACDC.

  • Patient-held consent

    Consent the person carries with them. They grant it, see it and withdraw it.

  • Referral and handover

    Structured hand-offs between disciplines, so context arrives with the person.

  • Delegated AI agents

    Agents work under a clinician's delegated, time-limited authority. Every action is signed.

Services

Help putting it to work.

People and processes that help practices adopt the protocols without disrupting care.

  • Practice onboarding

    Set up a clinic, a team or a sole practitioner, including identities and roles.

  • Clinical documentation

    Scribe support that drafts notes for the clinician to review and sign.

  • Privacy and compliance

    Australian Privacy Principles, AHPRA obligations and ISO 27001-aligned controls.

  • Interoperability

    Connect existing practice systems through FHIR AU and open APIs.

Tools

Instruments for the work itself.

Everyday tools for sessions, assessments and the routines people follow between visits.

  • Session notes

    Capture, review and sign notes in one place, with a clear record of who changed what.

  • Outcome measures

    K10, DASS-21 and PHQ-9 tracked over time, shared with the person's consent.

  • Developmental milestones

    Progress across speech, motor and social domains, visible to the whole care team.

  • Run sheets

    Step-by-step routines practitioners share with the people they support. See examples on selfdriven.health.
